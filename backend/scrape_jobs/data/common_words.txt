# The MIND Tech Ontology names real technologies after everyday words:
# After.js, Play Framework, Dropwizard Metrics, Query.jl, Page.js, PyTables,
# Room Persistence Library, Apache Pivot, Framer Motion. Importing their short
# synonyms verbatim taught the extractor that 56 Malaysian adverts wanted
# After.js, when what they said was "after".
#
# A term on this list is refused as a *bare* match only. The distinctive form
# always survives: "Spring Framework" stays active while "spring" does not,
# "Apache Kafka" while "kafka" is not even on this list. Nothing is deleted --
# a refused term stays in the catalogue, inactive, for a reviewer.
#
